Dr Suyi Ooi is neurologist with subspecialty in epilepsy and cognitive neurology. She works as a consultant neurologist and inaugural epileptologist at Bendigo Health. 

Suyi completed her PhD at the University of Melbourne under an NHMRC Postgraduate Scholarship, she used machine learning models and advanced neuroimaging to improve prediction of seizure recurrence after a first unprovoked seizure. Her research aims to develop clinically applicable neuroimaging tools that improve diagnosis, prognosis and treatment decisions for people with epilepsy.

Dr Ooi holds a Bachelor of Pharmacy (Honours) and a Bachelor of Medicine/Bachelor of Surgery at Deakin University. She has published in both national and international peer-reviewed journals.

Committed to improving access to specialist neurological care and research in regional Victoria, Dr Ooi has taken part in a Bendigo Health Foundation campaign highlighting these services.
